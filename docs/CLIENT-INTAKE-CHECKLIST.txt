CLIENT INTAKE CHECKLIST
=======================
What to collect from every client before building their site.


MUST-HAVE (Can't Launch Without)
================================

Business Identity
-----------------
- Business name (exact legal/brand name)
- Phone number (the one they actually answer)
- Street address (or "service-area only" if no storefront)
- City, state, zip
- Business hours (open/close times, which days)
- Years in business
- Owner/founder first name

Services
--------
- List of services they offer (need 4-6 for the page structure)
- Which service is their bread and butter (gets homepage emphasis)

Service Area
------------
- List of cities/towns they serve (need 8-12 for location pages)
- Primary city (homepage and SEO focus)

Reviews
-------
- Google Business Profile link (to pull real reviews, rating, review count)
- OR: screenshot their top 6-8 reviews with reviewer first name + last initial

Photos (minimum 4)
------------------
- 1 crew/team photo (in front of truck or on a job)
- 1 owner headshot or candid
- 2 before/after job photos
- Tell them: "Text me 4 photos from your phone -- your crew, yourself, and 2 job photos. That's it."

Domain
------
- Do they already have a domain? What is it?
- If not, what do they want? (e.g., smithjunkremoval.com)


NICE-TO-HAVE (Makes the Site Better)
=====================================

- Logo file (any format)
- Brand colors (if they care -- otherwise we pick)
- Tagline or slogan
- License/insurance details (license number, insured amount)
- Social media links (Facebook, Instagram, Yelp, Google)
- Specific neighborhoods they mention in their marketing
- Any awards or certifications
- "Origin story" -- how they started (2-3 sentences is enough)
- Additional job photos (the more the better, especially before/afters)


WHAT WE DON'T NEED FROM THEM
=============================

- Copy/text -- we write everything
- SEO keywords -- we research those
- Site structure decisions -- we own that
- Design preferences beyond colors -- we're the expert, that's what they're paying for
- FAQ answers -- we generate those from industry knowledge
- Competitor analysis -- we handle that


NOTES
=====

- Photos are always the hardest thing to get. Follow up on photos specifically.
- Most contractors won't write paragraphs. Keep questions short answer / multiple choice.
- Get the Google Business Profile link early -- it gives you reviews, rating, hours, address, and photos all in one place.
- If they don't have a Google Business Profile, flag that. They need one before the site goes live for local SEO to work.
