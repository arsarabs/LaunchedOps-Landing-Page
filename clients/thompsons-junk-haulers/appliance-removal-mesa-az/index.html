Professional Appliance Removal You Can Count On

When it is time to upgrade your kitchen or replace that aging water heater, the last thing you want to worry about is how to get rid of the old one. Thompson's Junk Haulers provides full-service appliance removal in Mesa, AZ and throughout the East Valley. We handle refrigerators, washers, dryers, ovens, stoves, dishwashers, water heaters, window AC units, and more. Whether it is a single appliance or a full kitchen remodel cleanout, our crew shows up on time, loads everything onto our truck, and hauls it away the same day.

Appliance removal is not as simple as dragging something to the curb. Many appliances contain hazardous materials that require special handling. Refrigerators and air conditioning units contain Freon refrigerant that must be recovered by certified technicians before disposal. Old appliances may also contain oils, mercury switches, or other components that cannot go into a standard landfill. Our team is trained to handle these materials properly, ensuring your appliance is disposed of in full compliance with Arizona environmental regulations.

We also go the extra mile with disconnection assistance. If your old washer still has hoses connected or your dryer is plugged into a 240-volt outlet, we can help with basic disconnections so you do not have to call a separate handyman. Once the appliance is out, we sweep up and leave the area clean. We recycle metals, donate working units to local charities when possible, and ensure responsible disposal for everything else. Need a hand with furniture removal at the same time? We can bundle services into a single trip to save you money. Planning a full remodel? Check out our full property cleanout service or our construction debris removal for a complete solution.
