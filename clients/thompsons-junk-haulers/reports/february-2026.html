Expanding to neighboring Scottsdale and Mesa with dedicated service area pages will capture additional local search volume and further increase organic traffic by an estimated 15-20%.

Increasing the QR code placement frequency and sending a follow-up text after each completed job should help reach the 25-review milestone, which is a key threshold for improved Google Maps visibility.
