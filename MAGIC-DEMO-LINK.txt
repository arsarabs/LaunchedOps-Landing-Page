MAGIC DEMO LINK — How It Works
===============================

The magic demo link is our closer. While you're on the phone with a prospect, you text them a URL that shows a fully built website with THEIR business name and city already on it. They open it, see their name in the hero, their city in the headlines, and a site that makes their current Wix/GoDaddy page look like garbage. The demo sells itself.


HOW IT WORKS
------------

One template per niche. No custom builds per prospect. URL parameters swap in their info everywhere on the page — business name, city, phone number. Same site, infinite personalized demos.

Example: You're calling "Timber Creek Tree Service" in Austin, TX.

You text them this link:

  https://demo.launchedops.com/tree-service?name=Timber%20Creek%20Tree%20Service&city=Austin&phone=5125551234

They open it on their phone. The hero says "Timber Creek Tree Service." The headline says "Everything your Austin trees need." The footer, the CTA buttons, the FAQ — all say Austin. It looks like you spent 3 days building a site just for them.

You didn't. You spent 0 seconds. The URL did it.


URL PARAMETERS
--------------

These go after the ? in the URL. Separate each one with &.

  name     Business name (shows everywhere — hero, footer, copyright, etc.)
  city     Their city (headings, FAQ, form section, footer)
  phone    Their phone number, 10 digits no dashes (all call buttons + footer)
  rating   Google rating, e.g. 4.8 (hero badge + reviews section)
  reviews  Review count, e.g. 150+ (hero badge + reviews heading)
  jobs     Jobs completed stat, e.g. 5,000+ (hero sidebar)
  r1       First reviewer name, e.g. Mike R.
  r2       Second reviewer name
  r3       Third reviewer name

Minimum for a cold call demo: name and city. That's it. Phone is a nice bonus.

Example with just the essentials:

  ?name=Portland%20Tree%20Pros&city=Portland

Example with everything:

  ?name=Timber%20Creek%20Tree%20Service&city=Austin&phone=5125551234&rating=4.9&reviews=85&jobs=2,000%2B&r1=Tom%20H.&r2=Karen%20S.&r3=Miguel%20R.


ENCODING RULES
--------------

Spaces become %20 in URLs. So "Big Oak Tree Service" becomes Big%20Oak%20Tree%20Service.

The + sign in things like "500+" needs to be encoded as %2B.

Commas in numbers like "2,000" need to be %2C or you can just write them without commas.

Most phones and browsers handle this automatically if you type it into a URL shortener.


THE COLD CALL FLOW
------------------

1. You're on the phone. You've already pointed out their slow load time, missing mobile CTA, or no Google reviews showing up.

2. You say: "Hey, I actually mocked something up for you — can I text you a link real quick? It's just a demo, not a finished site, but I want you to see what this could look like."

3. While they say yes, you're building the URL in your head (or a saved template with their name pre-filled).

4. Text the link. Stay on the phone.

5. They open it. They see their name. They see their city. They see a site that's faster, cleaner, and more professional than anything they've ever had.

6. Now you're not selling. You're just answering questions about something they can already see.

WHAT'S INTENTIONALLY NOT IN THE DEMO
-------------------------------------

- No crew/team photos (would look fake with stock photos of random people)
- No company timeline/history (fake founding dates break the illusion)
- No social proof toasts ("Someone just requested a quote!" — feels scammy)
- No company-specific testimonial names (reviews say "they" not the company name)

These sections get added when someone actually pays and we build the real site with their actual photos, actual team, actual reviews.


PRO TIPS
--------

- Use a URL shortener (bit.ly, short.io) so the text message doesn't look like a wall of code
- Pre-build links for your top 10-15 calls before you start dialing
- If you know their Google rating, include it — seeing their real rating on the demo is a killer detail
- The site is mobile-first. Most prospects will open on their phone. That's by design.
- If they ask "is this my site?" — say "this is a demo of what yours would look like. If you want to move forward, we customize everything — your real photos, your real reviews, your colors."
