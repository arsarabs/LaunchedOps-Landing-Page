21st.dev Component Recommendations for LaunchedOps
===================================================

Browse these on 21st.dev. When you find one you like, send me
the code and I'll adapt it to the Junk Moose site.

HIGHEST PRIORITY — Would immediately improve the site
------------------------------------------------------

Heroes (73 available)
  Look for: Dark hero with background image + overlay text,
  split-screen hero with image left / text right, or hero
  with scroll-triggered text reveal. Skip any with purple
  gradients or centered-everything layouts.

Testimonials (15 available)
  Look for: Large pull-quote testimonials, marquee/carousel
  testimonials, or card-based with photos. Anything that's
  NOT two cards side by side.

Calls to Action (34 available)
  Look for: Split CTA (phone left, form right), full-width
  banner CTA, or floating/sticky CTA bar. The current quote
  section could use a more creative layout.

Scroll Areas (24 available)
  Look for: Horizontal scroll sections, parallax scroll
  effects, or scroll-triggered number counters. These would
  make the Process or Stats section feel more alive.

Backgrounds (33 available)
  Look for: Noise/grain textures, mesh gradients, or subtle
  animated particle fields. Anything that adds depth to the
  dark sections without being distracting.


HIGH PRIORITY — Strong upgrades
------------------------------------------------------

Accordions (40 available)
  Look for: Animated accordions with smooth open/close,
  side-panel accordions, or accordions with icons. The FAQ
  section is solid but could be more distinctive.

Buttons (130 available)
  Look for: Magnetic hover buttons, border-draw buttons,
  shimmer/glow buttons, or buttons with micro-animations.
  Replace the current CTA button style.

Navigation Menus (11 available)
  Look for: Dark transparent nav with blur backdrop, mega
  menu for services, or hamburger menu with full-screen
  overlay animation.

Comparisons (6 available)
  Look for: Before/after image slider with drag handle.
  Would completely transform the Before & After section
  from static images to an interactive comparison.

Numbers (18 available)
  Look for: Animated number counters, odometer-style
  tickers, or large stat displays with labels. Would
  upgrade the Stats section.


MEDIUM PRIORITY — Nice-to-have polish
------------------------------------------------------

Texts (58 available)
  Look for: Text reveal animations, gradient text effects,
  typing/typewriter effects, or split-line text animations.
  Could spice up the hero headline.

Toasts (2 available)
  Look for: Anything more creative than the current social
  proof toast. Even with only 2, check if either fits.

Cards (79 available)
  Look for: Hover-tilt cards, glass cards, or cards with
  border-gradient effects. Would improve service cards.

Images (26 available)
  Look for: Image zoom on hover, tilt/parallax image
  effects, or image reveal animations. For the Before/After
  and founder photo sections.

Forms (23 available)
  Look for: Floating label inputs, animated focus states,
  or multi-step form wizards. Polish the quote form.

Footers (14 available)
  Look for: Dark minimal footer, footer with sitemap
  columns, or footer with newsletter CTA. The current
  footer is functional but could be more distinctive.

HOW TO USE THIS LIST
------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to 21st.dev
2. Browse the categories above in priority order
3. When you find a component you like, copy the code
4. Send it to me and say "use this for [section name]"
