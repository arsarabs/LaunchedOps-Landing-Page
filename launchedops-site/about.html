About

Built by Someone Who Gets It

I'm Anthony. I'm a computer science student and I've spent the last three years working in restaurants while building this business on the side. I started LaunchedOps because I kept seeing the same thing — contractors doing incredible work but losing jobs because their online presence was terrible.

A bad website. No Google reviews. Invisible on search. Meanwhile, their competitors with half the skill were getting all the calls because they looked better online.

That didn't sit right with me.

I build every site myself. I'm not a big agency with 50 employees. I'm one person who obsesses over every detail — the speed, the SEO, the design, the conversion.

Every site I build is something I'd be proud to put my name on. I don't cut corners. I don't use some drag-and-drop builder and call it custom. I write the code. I set up the SEO. I make sure it loads fast, looks right, and actually makes your phone ring.

My goal is simple: build you a website that makes your phone ring. If it doesn't, I haven't done my job.
