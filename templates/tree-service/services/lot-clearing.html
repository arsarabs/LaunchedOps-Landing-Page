Complete lot clearing for Portland construction & development

Portland's development market is active, and builders need lots that are clear, graded, and ready for construction. Whether you are a homeowner preparing a lot for a new build, a developer starting a multi-unit project, or a property owner reclaiming an overgrown parcel, Summit Tree Experts delivers turn-key lot clearing services that get your project moving on schedule.

We approach lot clearing differently than most tree services. Rather than bringing in heavy equipment that compacts soil and damages everything in its path, we use a combination of precision tree removal, brush cutting, stump grinding, and targeted chipping that leaves your site clean without unnecessary disruption. This matters in Portland, where environmental regulations mean soil compaction and erosion can trigger additional permitting requirements.

Our lot clearing service covers everything from a single residential lot in inner Southeast Portland to multi-acre parcels on the edges of the metro area. We have cleared land for custom homes in the West Hills, townhouse developments in St. Johns, ADU construction in Sellwood, and commercial projects in the Gateway district.

What our lot clearing service includes

Every lot clearing project is different, but our standard service covers the full scope of work needed to deliver a build-ready site:

  • Removal of all trees on the site (or selective preservation of trees you want to keep)
  • Stump grinding below grade for all removed trees
  • Brush, undergrowth, and blackberry removal
  • On-site chipping of all green waste
  • Hauling of all debris that cannot be processed on-site
  • Basic grading and leveling to leave a clean, even surface

We also handle all required Portland tree removal permits, coordinate with your general contractor or builder on timing and site access, and provide documentation of all trees removed for your development records. If your project requires a tree preservation plan, our arborist can identify which trees are worth saving and install protective fencing before work begins.

Portland permitting for lot clearing projects

Lot clearing in Portland involves navigating the city's tree code, which is one of the most detailed in the Pacific Northwest. Trees 12 inches or larger in diameter require a removal permit from the Bureau of Development Services. Environmental overlay zones — which cover significant portions of Portland near streams, wetlands, and steep slopes — have additional restrictions that can affect your clearing scope.

If your lot clearing is part of a permitted development project, tree removal is typically included in your land use review or building permit. For standalone clearing projects, separate tree removal permits are required. In either case, Portland often requires mitigation — planting replacement trees or paying into the city's tree fund — for each significant tree removed.

We handle the entire permitting process for our clients. We inventory every tree on the lot, determine which require permits, file the applications, and manage any required mitigation. This expertise saves our clients weeks of time and ensures there are no compliance surprises during construction.

Residential vs. commercial lot clearing

Residential lot clearing in Portland typically involves a standard city lot (5,000-7,500 square feet) with a handful of mature trees and varying amounts of undergrowth. These projects are completed in 1-3 days and are priced based on the number and size of trees, amount of brush, and site accessibility. Most residential lots in Portland cost between $3,000 and $10,000 to clear, depending on tree count and complexity.

Commercial and multi-lot clearing projects require more planning, heavier equipment, and longer timelines. We work directly with developers and general contractors to schedule clearing within the broader construction timeline. For these projects, we can mobilize larger crews and bring in specialized equipment like skid steers and full-size chippers to handle higher volume efficiently.

Regardless of project size, we maintain Portland's environmental standards on every job. All green waste is recycled, soil erosion controls are implemented when needed, and we protect any adjacent properties and trees that are not part of the clearing scope.
