Portland's storms don't wait — and neither do we

Portland's weather is beautiful most of the year, but when storms hit, they hit hard. The Willamette Valley's notorious wind events — including the Columbus Day Storm legacy and annual fall windstorms — can bring gusts exceeding 60 mph through the Portland metro area. Add ice storms, heavy snow events, and the occasional atmospheric river dumping inches of rain on already-saturated soil, and you have a recipe for tree failures across the city.

When a tree falls on your property, every minute matters. A tree on your roof means water is pouring into your home. A tree across your driveway means you cannot get to work or get your kids to school. A tree leaning on power lines means live electrical hazards in your yard. These are not situations that can wait until Monday morning.

Summit Tree Experts maintains an emergency crew on call around the clock. When you call our emergency line at (503) 555-0187, you reach a real person — not a voicemail, not an answering service. We assess your situation over the phone, dispatch a crew, and are typically on-site within 1-4 hours depending on conditions.

What we handle in emergency situations

Our emergency tree service in Portland covers any situation where a tree poses an immediate threat to people, property, or access. Here is what we respond to:

  • Trees fallen on homes, garages, sheds, and other structures
  • Trees blocking driveways, roads, and access points
  • Trees leaning on or tangled in power lines
  • Partially uprooted trees that could fall at any moment
  • Storm-split trees with hanging limbs over walkways
  • Trees threatening neighboring properties

We arrive with the equipment needed for the situation — chainsaws, rigging gear, chippers, and if necessary, we can mobilize a crane within hours. Our crew is trained specifically for emergency scenarios where speed and safety have to coexist.

Insurance claims — we handle the paperwork

If a tree has fallen on an insured structure — your home, detached garage, fence, or vehicle — your homeowner's insurance policy almost always covers the removal cost. We have worked with every major insurance company in the Portland market and understand exactly what documentation they need to process your claim quickly.

Here is what we provide for your insurance claim: detailed before-and-after photos documenting the damage and our work, an itemized invoice breaking down labor, equipment, and disposal costs, a written scope of work describing what was done and why, and direct communication with your adjuster if needed. We have helped hundreds of Portland homeowners navigate the insurance process, and we know how to document our work so your claim goes smoothly.

Important to know: trees that fall in your yard without hitting a structure may have limited or no coverage under your policy. Some policies include a small amount (often $500-$1,000) for tree removal even without structural damage, but this varies. We can help you understand your coverage before we begin work.

What to do when a tree falls on your property

A tree falling on your property is stressful, but staying calm and following these steps will help protect your family and your insurance claim:

First, ensure safety. Keep everyone — including pets — away from the tree and any downed power lines. Maintain at least 35 feet of distance from any wires on the ground. If the tree has hit your home, do not enter the damaged area until it has been assessed by a professional.

Second, call for help. If power lines are down, call PGE at 503-464-7777. If anyone is injured, call 911. Then call Summit Tree Experts at (503) 555-0187 for emergency tree removal.

Third, document the damage. Once it is safe, take photos from multiple angles. Photograph the tree, the damage to your property, and the surrounding area. This documentation is critical for your insurance claim. Do not move or cut anything before taking photos.

Fourth, contact your insurance company. File your claim as soon as possible. Let them know you have a tree service en route and that you will provide documentation of the removal work.
