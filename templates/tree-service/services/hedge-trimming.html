Expert hedge maintenance for Portland properties

Hedges are the backbone of Portland's residential landscapes. They provide privacy, define property boundaries, block wind, reduce noise, and create the lush green backdrop that makes Portland neighborhoods so appealing. But without regular maintenance, hedges quickly become overgrown, leggy, and unattractive — creating the opposite of the polished look you want.

At Summit Tree Experts, hedge trimming is not an afterthought — it is a core service performed by skilled crew members who understand plant growth habits and proper cutting technique. We do not just hack at your hedges with a power trimmer. We assess each hedge's species, growth pattern, and desired shape, then use the right combination of hand shears and power trimmers to achieve clean, even lines that promote dense, healthy growth from the base to the top.

Our hedge trimming services cover the entire Portland metro area, from formal boxwood borders in Eastmoreland to towering laurel hedges in the West Hills. Whether you need a one-time cleanup or year-round maintenance, we deliver results that make your property look its best.

Portland's most popular hedge species

Portland's mild, wet climate supports an incredible variety of hedge plants. Each species has unique growth habits and maintenance requirements. Here are the most common types we work with:

  • English laurel — fast-growing, dense privacy hedge; needs 2-3 trims per year to stay controlled
  • Portuguese laurel — more refined than English laurel; slower growth, less aggressive trimming schedule
  • Boxwood — the classic formal hedge; slow-growing, responds well to precise shaping
  • Yew — excellent for shading areas; very tolerant of heavy pruning and reshaping
  • Arborvitae — popular privacy screen; needs careful trimming to avoid bare spots
  • Privet — vigorous grower; requires frequent trimming but recovers well from neglect
  • Holly — traditional Portland hedge; sharp leaves require careful handling

Each species has an optimal trimming schedule and technique. Our crew knows the difference, and that knowledge is what separates a professional result from a DIY attempt.

Overgrown hedge restoration in Portland

Have your hedges gotten away from you? Maybe you bought a home with neglected landscaping, or you have been too busy to keep up with trimming, or a previous landscaper did a poor job. Whatever the reason, overgrown hedges are one of the most common issues we address for Portland homeowners.

Restoring an overgrown hedge is not as simple as cutting it back hard. Many species — especially arborvitae and certain conifers — will not regrow from old wood. Cut them back too far and you are left with bare, brown patches that never fill in. Our arborists know which species tolerate heavy reduction and which require a gradual approach over multiple seasons.

For species that tolerate hard pruning (like laurel, yew, and privet), we can often restore the hedge to its desired size in a single visit. For more sensitive species, we develop a 2-3 year restoration plan that gradually brings the hedge back to size while maintaining its green exterior. This patient approach costs less in the long run than ripping out and replanting.

Maintenance plans for Portland homeowners

The best way to keep your hedges looking great is to prevent them from becoming overgrown in the first place. Our annual maintenance plans take the guesswork out of hedge care. We schedule your trims at the optimal times for your specific hedge species, show up on schedule, and handle all cleanup.

A typical maintenance plan includes 2-3 visits per year, timed to your hedge species and Portland's growing season. For fast growers like English laurel and privet, we may recommend monthly visits during the peak growing season from May through September. For slower growers like boxwood and yew, two well-timed trims per year are sufficient.

Maintenance plan clients receive priority scheduling (helpful during Portland's busy spring season), consistent pricing with no surprises, and the peace of mind that comes from knowing your hedges are being cared for by professionals who understand them. Most of our Portland hedge clients have been with us for years — they set it and forget it while their hedges stay perfect.

// FAQ

Hedge trimming questions.

Common questions about hedge trimming in Portland, OR. Still have questions? Get in touch.

Most hedges need trimming 2-3 times per year. Fast-growing species like laurel and privet may need trimming every 6-8 weeks during the growing season (April-September). Slower growers like boxwood and yew need just 1-2 trims. We offer maintenance plans that keep hedges looking sharp year-round.

For most evergreen hedges, the first trim should happen in late spring (May-June) after the main growth flush, with a follow-up in late summer (August-September). Deciduous hedges are best trimmed in late winter while dormant and again mid-summer. Avoid trimming in late fall, as new growth may not harden before Portland's first frosts.

We trim all hedge types common to Portland: English laurel, Portuguese laurel, boxwood, yew, arborvitae, privet, photinia, holly, escallonia, and mixed native hedgerows. We also handle tall hedge reduction, hedge restoration for neglected properties, and formal topiary shaping.

Hedge trimming in Portland typically costs $150-$500 per visit depending on total linear footage, hedge height, and species. Tall hedges requiring ladder work cost more than routine maintenance. We offer annual maintenance plans with 2-3 visits at a discounted rate. Every estimate is free and on-site.
