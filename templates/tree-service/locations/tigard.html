Tree Service in Tigard, OR

From Bull Mountain to Cook Park, Tigard properties need expert tree care. Summit Tree Experts provides ISA-certified removal, trimming, and 24/7 emergency service throughout Tigard.

Get Free Estimate → Call Now

Tigard straddles the Tualatin Valley between Portland and the Coast Range foothills, giving the city a diverse tree landscape that ranges from dense conifer stands on Bull Mountain to riparian corridors of alder and willow along Fanno Creek. Neighborhoods built during the 1980s and 90s are now dealing with trees that have outgrown their original planting spaces — Douglas firs towering over rooftops, root systems buckling driveways, and canopies blocking solar panels and gutters.

The Fanno Creek watershed runs through the heart of Tigard, and the trees along its banks play a critical role in erosion control and habitat. However, saturated soils along the creek corridor make red alders and Oregon ash trees prone to root failure and sudden windthrow. Summit Tree Experts has extensive experience working in Tigard's sensitive lands areas, performing hazard tree removals while maintaining compliance with Clean Water Services and city environmental regulations.

Tigard's location in the Tualatin Valley makes it particularly vulnerable to channeled windstorms that sweep down from the Coast Range. Bull Mountain properties sit exposed on a ridge, and tall Douglas firs and western red cedars on the summit regularly suffer wind damage during fall and winter storms. Our emergency crews know Tigard's geography and respond quickly when storms hit.

// Tigard Tree Service FAQ

Common questions about Tigard tree service.

Does Tigard have tree removal regulations?

Tigard regulates tree removal through its Community Development Code. Trees over 12 inches in diameter generally require city review before removal. Tigard also has specific protections for trees in sensitive lands, flood plains, and along Fanno Creek. Summit Tree Experts manages all permit requirements for Tigard tree removal projects.

What tree issues are common in Tigard neighborhoods?

Tigard's Bull Mountain area sees frequent Douglas fir removals due to trees planted too close to homes during original development. Along Fanno Creek, red alders and willows often become hazardous from root rot in saturated soils. The Summerlake and Cook Park neighborhoods deal with large cottonwoods that drop massive amounts of cotton and debris each spring.

How much does stump grinding cost in Tigard?

Stump grinding in Tigard typically costs $150-$500 per stump depending on diameter, root flare, and access. We grind 6-8 inches below grade and can haul away the chips or leave them for fill. Multi-stump discounts are available — ask about pricing when you request your free estimate.

Do you provide emergency tree service in Tigard?

Yes — Summit Tree Experts provides 24/7 emergency storm damage response in Tigard. Windstorms channeling through the Tualatin Valley regularly topple trees and snap large limbs, especially in exposed areas like Bull Mountain. We respond to emergencies involving trees on structures, vehicles, or blocking roadways within hours.
