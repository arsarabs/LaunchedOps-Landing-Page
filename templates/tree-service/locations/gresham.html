Tree Service in Gresham, OR

Gresham's proximity to the Cascades foothills means massive trees and serious storm exposure. Summit Tree Experts brings ISA-certified expertise and heavy equipment to every Gresham project.

Get Free Estimate → Call Now

Gresham is the fourth-largest city in Oregon and sits on the eastern edge of the Portland metro, where suburban neighborhoods transition into the wooded foothills of the Cascade Range. Properties in Pleasant Valley, Powell Valley, and the areas heading toward Boring often sit on multi-acre lots with stands of old-growth-scale Douglas fir, grand fir, and western hemlock. These are some of the tallest, most challenging trees our crews work on anywhere in the metro.

The east wind events that funnel through the Columbia River Gorge hit Gresham harder than most metro communities. These dry, high-velocity winds can gust over 60 mph and are responsible for significant tree damage every winter. Douglas firs with compromised root systems, dead tops, or heavy lean are particularly vulnerable. Gresham homeowners who haven't had their trees inspected are often surprised at the hazards an arborist assessment reveals.

Gresham tree service also involves navigating the city's urban-rural interface. The Rockwood and Centennial neighborhoods have typical suburban lots with aging ornamental trees and street plantings, while Kelly Creek and Hogan Cedars have larger parcels that may require lot clearing for new construction or fire mitigation. Summit Tree Experts handles the full spectrum — from a single ornamental removal in downtown Gresham to multi-day clearing projects on the east side.

// Gresham Tree Service FAQ

Common questions about Gresham tree service.

Does Gresham require tree removal permits?

Gresham regulates tree removal on developed properties. Trees over 8 inches in diameter within the city limits generally require a permit. Properties in the Pleasant Valley or Springwater planning areas may have additional tree preservation requirements. Summit Tree Experts handles all Gresham permit applications as part of our service.

What are the biggest tree concerns in Gresham?

Gresham's east-side location near the Cascades foothills means properties frequently have large native conifers — Douglas fir, western red cedar, and grand fir — that can reach 120+ feet. These massive trees pose significant risks during east wind events that funnel through the Columbia River Gorge. Gresham also sees heavy root damage to foundations and sewer lines from mature big-leaf maples.

How much does tree removal cost in Gresham?

Gresham tree removal costs range from $400 for small ornamental trees to $4,000-$7,000 for large conifers in the Powell Valley and Pleasant Valley areas. Costs depend on tree height, diameter, proximity to structures, and access. Gresham properties often have larger lots with better access than inner Portland, which can reduce costs. Free estimates available.

Do you serve the Boring and Pleasant Valley areas?

Yes. Our Gresham service area extends east through Pleasant Valley, Powell Valley, and into Boring. These semi-rural properties often have the largest trees and most complex removal projects in the metro area. We bring the heavy equipment needed for big timber — including crane service for trees that can't be conventionally climbed.
