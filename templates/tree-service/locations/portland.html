Tree Service in Portland, OR

Portland's dense urban canopy demands expert care. Summit Tree Experts delivers ISA-certified tree removal, trimming, and emergency storm response across every Portland neighborhood.

Get Free Estimate → Call Now

Portland is one of the most heavily treed cities in the nation, with an urban canopy that covers nearly 30% of the city. From towering Douglas firs in the West Hills to massive big-leaf maples lining streets in Laurelhurst and Irvington, the city's trees are a defining feature — and a constant source of maintenance challenges. Summit Tree Experts has served Portland homeowners and businesses for years, handling everything from routine pruning to hazardous removals in tight residential lots.

The Portland climate — wet winters followed by dry summers — creates a cycle that promotes rapid growth and then stresses trees through drought. Western red cedars, Oregon white oaks, and native hazelnuts all thrive here, but so do invasive species like English holly and tree-of-heaven that crowd out healthy growth. Ice storms, like the ones that regularly knock out power across Southeast and Northeast Portland, can cause catastrophic limb failure on trees that haven't been properly maintained.

Portland's tree removal regulations are among the strictest in Oregon. The city's Urban Forestry division requires permits for most removals, and certain heritage trees carry additional protections. Our team manages the permit process from start to finish, ensuring your project stays compliant with Title 11 tree code while protecting your property and your neighbors.

// Portland Tree Service FAQ

Common questions about Portland tree service.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Portland?

Portland requires a permit from the Urban Forestry department to remove most trees over 12 inches in diameter on private property. Summit Tree Experts handles the entire permit process for you, including the application and any required mitigation planting.

What trees cause the most problems in Portland?

Big-leaf maples and Douglas firs are the most common service calls in Portland. Maples develop heavy canopies that drop massive amounts of debris in fall, while older Douglas firs can become hazardous in windstorms. English ivy invasion is also a major issue that weakens and kills mature trees throughout the city.

How quickly can you respond to storm damage in Portland?

We provide 24/7 emergency storm damage response throughout Portland. During major storm events like ice storms or windstorms common to the Willamette Valley, we typically arrive within 2-4 hours for hazardous situations involving downed trees on structures or blocking roadways.

What does Portland tree removal typically cost?

Portland tree removal ranges from $500 for small trees to $5,000+ for large Douglas firs or complex removals near structures. We provide free on-site estimates with transparent pricing — no hidden fees. Factors include tree height, diameter, proximity to buildings, and whether a city permit is required.
